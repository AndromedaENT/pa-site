Personal Assistant Agent

About this app

Personal Assistant Agent is a personal productivity assistant that helps manage tasks, notes, and email through a conversational chat interface. It is a single-user application built and operated by its owner for personal use.

What it does with your Google account

  • Reads and searches your Gmail messages to help you find and summarize email
  • Drafts and sends email replies on your behalf, only when you request it
  • Does not share your data with any third party
  • Does not use your data for advertising
