Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Personal Assistant Agent

Overview

Personal Assistant Agent ("the app") is a personal productivity assistant. It is operated as a single-user application by its owner and is not offered as a public or commercial service. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Data we access

When connected to Google, the app may access:

  • Gmail message content and metadata, for search, summarization, and reply drafting
  • Basic Google account profile information (name and email address) used to identify the connected account

How data is used

  • Email content is processed to answer the user's requests within the chat interface (e.g. "find the email about X" or "draft a reply to Y")
  • Drafted or sent replies are only created when explicitly requested by the user
  • No data is used to train third-party or public machine learning models
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers

Data storage and security

  • OAuth credentials are stored server-side, encrypted at rest, and are never exposed to the browser or any client-side code
  • The application runs on private infrastructure controlled by the app owner
  • Access to stored data is restricted to the application's own backend services

Data sharing

The app does not share Google user data with any third party, except where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

Data retention and deletion

Google account access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions. To request deletion of any data associated with your account, contact us using the information below.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Personal Assistant Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or your data can be sent to sundevil93@gmail.com.
